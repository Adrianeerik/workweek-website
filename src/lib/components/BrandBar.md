# BrandBar

The bottom strip: "the workweek" logo, email and copyright. The copyright year updates automatically.

## Props
| Prop | Type | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tall` | `boolean` | `false` | 40vh-tall version used under the footer form. The short version is used on the contact page. |

## What it replaces
`.container.bg-green.content-vcenter.footer`, `.brand`, `.label.inverted`, and the separate `section small bg-green` on the contact page.
