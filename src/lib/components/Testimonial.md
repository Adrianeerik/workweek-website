# Testimonial

A review card: name, role, stars and quote. It's a semantic `<figure>` with `<figcaption>` and `<blockquote>`. The card doesn't position itself; whoever places it decides that (see Hero).

## Props
| Prop | Type |
|---|---|
| `name` | `string` |
| `role` | `string` |
| `quote` | `string` |

## What it replaces
`.card`, `.paragraph.large`, `.paragraph.small`, `.image.review`.
